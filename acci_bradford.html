Privacy Policy – ACCI Bradford Effective date: 13 September 2026 ACCI Bradford respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the ACCI Bradford mobile application (“the App”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the App. Information we collect The App may collect the following information: Name – used to identify you within your ACCI Bradford member account and relevant church features. Email address – used to create, authenticate and manage your account. User ID – a unique identifier is associated with your account to provide account functionality and securely associate information with the correct user. User-generated content – information that you voluntarily submit through the App, including prayer requests and content submitted to the Prayer Wall. Submission of this information is optional. Device or other identifiers – the App may use Firebase-generated installation or device identifiers to provide essential functionality, including push notifications. How we use your information We use information collected through the App to: create, authenticate and manage user accounts; provide the features and functionality of the ACCI Bradford App; receive and manage prayer requests and Prayer Wall submissions; provide church information and services; send relevant app and church notifications; and maintain the security and reliability of the App. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising and we do not sell users' personal information. Prayer requests and user-generated content Prayer requests are submitted voluntarily. Where the App provides a choice between a private prayer request and a Prayer Wall submission, users control which option they select. Content intended for the Prayer Wall may be reviewed or moderated before being displayed to other users. Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in prayer requests or other content submitted through the App. Third-party services ACCI Bradford uses Google Firebase services to support features such as authentication, cloud data storage and push notifications. Information may therefore be processed by Google as a service provider in accordance with applicable Firebase and Google privacy and security terms. The App may also provide links to external services such as YouTube, Facebook or Zoom. When users choose to access an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. Payments Online card payments are not currently available within this version of the ACCI Bradford App. The App does not currently collect or process users' payment-card information. Security We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. Information transmitted between the App and its cloud services is encrypted in transit. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure of personal information. Data retention Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain user accounts, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Account and data deletion Users may request deletion of their ACCI Bradford account and associated personal data. When an account deletion request is completed, personal data associated with the account will be deleted unless certain information must be retained for a legitimate legal, security or regulatory reason. Instructions for requesting account deletion are available through ACCI Bradford's account-deletion page and within the App. Children's privacy The App is intended for the ACCI Bradford church community. We do not knowingly use children's personal information for advertising or marketing. Where the App is made available to younger users, appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements will be followed. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available on this page.
