Voice and microphone BRYLOQ requests microphone access only when you choose to use voice input. Voice input is used to convert your spoken instructions into text for creating and organising tasks, reminders and plans. BRYLOQ does not continuously listen to or record your microphone in the background. This processing is used to interpret your instructions and generate structured productivity information such as tasks, dates, reminders and plans. Google Calendar Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you choose to connect it, BRYLOQ may access calendar events and may create or update calendar events as requested by you. Calendar information is used only to provide calendar and planning functionality. BRYLOQ does not sell calendar information or use it for advertising. Local storage Tasks, reminders, plans and related BRYLOQ information may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your information between sessions. You can remove locally stored app information by deleting items in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling BRYLOQ. Third-party services BRYLOQ uses Google and Firebase services, including Firebase AI Logic, Firebase App Check, Google Play Integrity, Google Sign-In and Google Calendar. These services may process information necessary to provide their functionality and are subject to their applicable privacy and data-processing terms. Data sharing BRYLOQ does not sell your personal information.
